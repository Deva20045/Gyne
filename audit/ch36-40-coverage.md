# Chapters 36–40: source-order coverage and review record

Date: 2026-10-27. Source: supplied Marrow OBG Edition 8 scans, Vol 1 part 05.

## Page-number convention

Existing `page` metadata uses the **scan sequence**, not the printed folio. For this batch, scan 193–221 = printed 185–213 = part-05 PDF pages 13–41. No pages from the following Contraception: Images chapter were incorporated.

## Method and limits

- Rasterised all 29 requested source sheets at 2×; OCR transcribed each sheet; visually read every sheet and compared headings, tables, arrows and figure captions against authoring blocks.
- Review revisited the full page topics in order; magnified implant-duration and cause-percentage crops resolved unclear handwriting. Raw OCR is retained separately as an aid, not authority.
- Every question is hand-authored with four explicit options and an explanation. The helper only parses authored text; it does not generate questions from OCR.
- Page/section order is preserved, including same-page continuation sections. Compound questions can cover multiple adjacent lines; there is not necessarily one question per printed line.
- This is an educational source-coverage review, not independent specialist medical validation. Historical thresholds, programme eligibility and potentially unsafe annotations are labelled in explanations. Use current local clinical guidance, not quiz answers, for care.

## Source discrepancies and cautions retained at their source location

- Scan 193: tubal cause is **second**, not the OCR reading “third”.
- Scan 195: maturation-index examples conflict with the defined A/B/C order; biopsy date is **day 26**, not OCR day 21. Source karyopyknotic expression is explicitly framed as drawn.
- Scan 197: “AMH from menarche to puberty” is chronologically erroneous; FSH/AFC/AMH source thresholds are not stand-alone contemporary POI diagnostic criteria.
- Scan 203: handwritten female-factor range is **40–35%**, not an invented 40–55%; 20% fecundability versus 50% live-birth fecundity for the same cycle is inconsistent. The age/waiting table is source-specific, not advice to delay evaluation over age 40.
- Scan 206: varicocele is placed in an obstructive branch but usually impairs testicular function; normal FSH alone does not prove obstruction.
- Scans 207–210: historical IUI prerequisite thresholds are distinguished from semen reference limits; “HCG to rule out tubal blockage” is clarified as HSG. Retrieval should precede spontaneous rupture. Multiple-embryo transfer is not promoted without the multiple-pregnancy caution.
- Scans 211–212: oversimplified mechanism/drug-interaction and BP eligibility claims are labelled. No blanket ban is applied to all antiepileptics or all POPs.
- Scan 213: “missed 1 or 2” overlaps “missed ≥2”; the contradiction is flagged. New packs follow their scheduled interval rather than waiting indefinitely for bleeding.
- Scans 214–216: source tumour associations, malposition/PID pathways and implant figures are contextualised; Actinomyces association is not equated with all PID, or asymptomatic cytology with clinical infection. The one-year Norplant/Jadelle annotation and fixed release-duration statement are flagged.
- Scan 217: source LNG minipill dose 35 micrograms is labelled, not presented as an unqualified prescription.
- Scan 218: historical programme criteria do not replace adult voluntary consent, capacity assessment, legal safeguards or appropriate medical/perioperative assessment.
- Scan 219: Essure is identified as historical, not offered as a newly available procedure.
- Scan 220: Yuzpe progestin name and reversed high-/low-dose tablet counts are flagged; dosing requires actual product contents. Emergency methods do not terminate an established pregnancy.
- Scan 221: source “never Mirena” is edition-specific; postpartum mnemonic is reconciled with immediate method-specific options; the DMPA osteoporosis timing annotation is not treated as a valid universal rule.
